Miso

An iPhone app for couples. Two people share one household in the app and see the same things.

What it does

  • Shared to-dos and shopping lists, with repeats and check-offs your partner sees.
  • Meal plans for the week.
  • Both of your calendars in one place, so each of you can see when the other is busy.
  • A weekly sleep, steps and chores matchup from Apple Health, Whoop or Oura.
  • Hugs, notes and nudges sent to your partner's phone.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Miso asks for read-only access to your calendars and uses it only to show your events in the app to you and your partner. It never creates, changes or deletes events. Google data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside your household. Miso's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Availability

Miso is in beta on Apple's TestFlight, by invitation.
