How do we share a household?

One of you taps Start a household. The other taps Join a household and enters the 6-character code from the first phone (Settings, or the invite step in setup), or opens the invite link.

My partner doesn't see my changes

Both phones need to be online. Changes made offline show an Offline tag and sync once you're connected. Pull down on Today to refresh.

Health numbers are missing

Check iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Miso and turn on the categories. Health is only read while your iPhone is unlocked, so new numbers appear after you open the app.

Google says the app isn't verified

Miso's Google verification is in progress. Until it's done, tap Advanced, then Go to Miso. Miso only reads your calendars and never changes them.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Delete account. It deletes your account and data right away. See the privacy policy for what's kept for your partner.
