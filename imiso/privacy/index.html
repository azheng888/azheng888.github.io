iMiso privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026.

iMiso is a private app used by one household. It has no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Google Calendar

When you connect your Google account, iMiso gets read-only access to your calendars (the calendar.readonly scope). It reads your events to show them in the app, and shares a copy of your upcoming events (title, time, location, calendar name and color) with the other person in your household through Apple iCloud. It never creates, changes or deletes events.

Your Google sign-in is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. Disconnect any time in the app's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

iMiso's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside your household.

Other data

To-dos, notes, daily health summaries and spending totals are stored in your household's iCloud (Apple CloudKit) and shared only between the household's two phones.
